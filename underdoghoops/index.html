Meet Your Coach

Coach Sahil
Prabhu

Coach Sahil founded Underdog Hoops in 2023 after nearly a decade of competitive basketball experience. He has started at point guard for his high school varsity team for three consecutive years and named a Top 10 Player in his school district. He also has the school record for assist in a game with 16.

His coaching approach combines detailed skill development with demanding, encouraging sessions that help young athletes expand what they believe they can accomplish on the court and beyond it.

Coaching is kept affordable so cost stays a small hurdle, not a wall—and a portion of every fee is donated so the impact reaches even further.

Why We Started

More Indian Kids
In the Game

Underdog Hoops was created in part to help more Indian and South Asian youth feel welcomed, confident, and represented in sports.

Our mission is to show families that athletics can grow alongside academics. Basketball can build discipline, leadership, health, friendships, resilience, and the confidence to take on challenges in every part of life.

This mission is rooted in the Indian community, but every session and camp is open to young athletes from all backgrounds.

Affordable, Not Out of ReachCoaching is priced to stay accessible, and four free community camps run every month so cost never sidelines a willing player.
Representation MattersYoung Indian and South Asian athletes deserve to see people from their communities coaching, competing, and leading.
Sports Build More Than SkillThe goal is stronger players, healthier habits, supportive friendships, and confident young people.

Giving Back

We Play
For More

10% of every coaching fee goes to Good Sports — giving every kid the chance to play.

A portion of every coaching fee at Underdog Hoops is donated to Good Sports, a nonprofit that provides brand-new sports equipment, apparel, and footwear to youth in high-need communities.

For too many kids, a missing pair of shoes or a worn-out ball is the reason they never get to play. Good Sports removes that barrier—so the underdog spirit that drives this program reaches far beyond one court in Frisco.

Common Questions

FAQ

What ages and skill levels do you train?

Underdog Hoops works with youth players at different stages of development. Include the player’s age and experience in your inquiry so Coach Sahil can recommend the right option.

Where are sessions held?

Sessions are held in the Frisco, Texas area. The exact facility and arrival details are confirmed during scheduling.

How much does coaching cost?

Personal coaching is kept affordable, and exact rates are shared when you reach out. On top of that, four community camps are hosted completely free every month.

Where does the donation go?

A portion of every coaching fee is donated to Good Sports, a nonprofit that provides sports equipment and gear to kids in high-need communities so more young athletes can play.

Is Underdog Hoops only for Indian or South Asian players?

No. The mission includes encouraging greater sports participation in the Indian and South Asian community, but training is open to young athletes from every background.

How do the free camps work?

Four free camps run each month. Details and sign-up information are shared before each one—use the form below and select “Free Monthly Camps” to be notified.
